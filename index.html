The example · Planning change

School life changes the plan.

Before making a change, understand its chance of success. Ptolemy helps you stress-test an initiative against your school’s capacity to deliver successful change.

  • Can we introduce the initiative without removing other tasks?
  • What existing tasks could be reduced or removed to make it feasible?
  • Who has the capacity to lead or trial the new approach?
  • Where will the impact fall across different roles?

This is a small example of some of the ways Ptolemy can help your work—only a snapshot of its wider functionality.
